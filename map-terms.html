Map terms and privacy

Google Maps terms

Papalita Rider uses Google Maps to display streets and delivery stops. By using the in-app map, you agree to the Google Maps and Google Earth Additional Terms of Service.

Location and map requests

Google processes map requests and viewport information according to the Google Privacy Policy. When you tap My location or Show my location and allow location access, Papalita uses your GPS position to show where you are on the map.

When you tap Share live GPS after pickup, Papalita sends your position to the delivery tracking service so the customer can follow the delivery. To stop sharing, tap Stop sharing GPS.

Show road route sends the start and destination coordinates to the routing provider to calculate a route. A Directions link opens Google Maps with the selected destination.
